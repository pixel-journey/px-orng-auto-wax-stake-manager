# Education

Free adaptive credits are the cheapest path. Paid credits are next. Self-staking WAX for free RNG is a last resort that permanently commits capital to the RNG service.

The planner refuses to stake when free or paid already cover the need. The stake body is **unsigned and unsent** until a real transfer is built by a later shell.

Lab scale: 1 unit of stake → 1 free credit. Real WAX precision and orng rates are not claimed here.
